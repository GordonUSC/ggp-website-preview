The story

A community first. A movement, always.

GGP didn't start as a nonprofit. It started as a room — and then a hallway, and then a whole hidden network of queer people in games who refused to keep building this industry alone.

Mission

To promote, cultivate, and unite the professional LGBTQ+ community in gaming.

Twenty years on, the mission still reads like the manifesto it was at the kitchen table where it started. Three verbs. One community. A lifetime of work.

Promote — visibility for queer creators in games, on the record, on stages, in the credits.

Cultivate — pipelines, scholarships, mentorship, livestreams, and a Twitch creator hub that turns talent into careers.

Unite — a real network. Not a logo. Not a Pride campaign. A bench-deep community that knows each other's names and picks up the phone.

"Promote, cultivate, unite — the verbs are the whole strategy."

— GGP, founding mission
Timeline

Twenty years, one through-line.

2006

The community begins

Gordon Bellamy founds Gay Gaming Professionals as a meetup-and-mailing-list — queer industry pros finding each other inside a games industry that was still pretending we weren't there.

2008–2017

The hallway years

For more than a decade, GGP runs as community: GDC late-night meetups, listserv mentorship, in-DM career help. No formal entity. No fundraising. Just the work.

2018

501(c)(3) incorporation

GG Professionals Inc. is recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 83-2884652). The handshake becomes infrastructure. The mission stays exactly the same.

2019–2022

Programs ship

Take-Two DEI partnership launches. Core Videos library begins documenting queer industry leaders on the record. Collegiate Games Challenge surfaces LGBTQ+ student devs at scale.

2023–2025

The wider network

GGP becomes a node in a multi-org coalition with Games for Change, GLAAD Gaming, IGDA's LGBTQ+ SIG, and dozens of studio partners. The Twitch Affiliate Hub formalizes creator-economy support.

2026

Year twenty

20 years of community. The work continues — louder, queerer, more strategically resourced, and as personal as it ever was.

Founder

Gordon Bellamy

Gordon Bellamy is a games industry veteran whose career has spanned EA Sports (where he led Madden NFL development), Tencent, MTV, GameTrailers, and the Game Developers Conference. He served as Executive Director of the IGDA, sits on the boards of Games for Change and the ESA Foundation, and is a faculty member at the USC School of Cinematic Arts where he teaches Anatomy of the Game and Intro to Streaming.

He founded Gay Gaming Professionals in 2006 — the first community of its kind in the games industry — and has spent the last two decades building, mentoring, and championing queer talent in interactive entertainment.

A Harvard alum and a longtime Los Angeles resident, Gordon has been honored by Games for Change, NZXT, and the ESA Foundation for his contributions to the industry and the community he built within it.

"Build the room you needed when you got here. Then leave the door wide open."

— Gordon Bellamy, Founder
