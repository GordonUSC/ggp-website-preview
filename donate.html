Donor FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is GGP a registered 501(c)(3)?

Yes — GG Professionals Inc. is a federally-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity. Our EIN is 83-2884652. All US donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

How are my dollars used?

The majority of every dollar goes to direct programmatic spending — scholarships, internship stipends, creator equipment grants, mentorship logistics, and content production. We're a small, lean nonprofit; overhead is intentionally minimized.

Can I designate my gift?

Yes. The donation form lets you choose between general support and specific designations (scholarships, Collegiate Games Challenge, Twitch Affiliate Hub, etc.). Designated funds are restricted to that program.

Do you accept gifts of stock or DAF grants?

Yes. We accept appreciated securities and donor-advised-fund grants. Contact us for transfer instructions.

Will I receive a tax receipt?

Yes. Every donation receives an emailed receipt with our EIN, the gift amount, and a confirmation that no goods or services were received in exchange — suitable for your tax records.

Can my company match my gift?

Many companies match charitable contributions. Check your employer's matching gift program — most platforms (Benevity, YourCause, etc.) already have GGP listed under "GG Professionals Inc." If not, our EIN 83-2884652 is what you'll need.
