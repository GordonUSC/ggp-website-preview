For students & early-career

Apply to a GGP program.

If you're an LGBTQ+ student or early-career professional in games, GGP has multiple ways in. Applications open on a rolling basis — submit the form and we'll route you to the right cohort or scholarship cycle.

Open programs:

  • Collegiate Games Challenge — submit a playable game project
  • Advanced Android Makers — mobile-shipping cohort
  • Take-Two DEI Internship Pipeline — paid internships at Take-Two studios
  • Twitch Affiliate Hub — creator support

For studios

Build a real LGBTQ+ pipeline.

If you're at a studio and you want your DEI work to be more than a Pride-month logo swap, GGP is the partner. We co-design internship pipelines, hiring referral programs, employee-resource-group support, and inclusive-development consulting.

Our flagship industry partnership with Take-Two has been running for years and has placed real talent into Rockstar, 2K, and Zynga. We replicate that model with the right partners.

What partnership looks like:

  • Co-branded internship and full-time hiring pipelines
  • ERG support, training, and live programming
  • Inclusive-development consulting on in-game representation
  • Honors and program sponsorship in your studio's name

For brands & foundations

Sponsor a program. Move the needle.

Brand sponsorship of GGP programming directly funds scholarships, internship stipends, creator support, and the GGP Honors ceremony. Every sponsorship dollar lands on a person — not a slide deck.

Sponsorship inventory:

  • GGP Honors title sponsorship and category sponsorships
  • Collegiate Games Challenge prize underwriting
  • Twitch Affiliate Hub creator stipend funds
  • Core Videos production sponsorship
  • Advanced Android Makers cohort underwriting

All sponsorships are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. EIN 83-2884652.

For working pros

Volunteer your time.

If you're a working professional in games — at any level, any discipline — GGP needs you. Our most impactful programs are built on a bench of mentors, jurors, and panelists who show up.

Where mentors are needed:

  • Collegiate Games Challenge jury (1–2 hour reviews)
  • Advanced Android Makers technical mentorship (cohort-based)
  • Twitch Affiliate Hub creator coaching
  • Resume reviews and mock interviews for student applicants
  • Core Videos guest interviews

You don't need to be queer to volunteer. You do need to be in the work.
