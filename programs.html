Programs

Six programs. One mission.

Every program GGP runs is built to deliver concrete outcomes against the four E's — Education, Expertise, Employment, Entrepreneurship. These are the in-flight initiatives.

Jump to programs ↓
01
Cohort

Advanced Android Makers

Education Entrepreneurship Mobile

A skills-and-shipping cohort for queer mobile developers. Participants ship a real Android title — game engine, store presence, monetization, and the business side of indie publishing. Mentors come from inside the industry. The deliverable is a live game.

Open to early-career LGBTQ+ developers and student teams. Cohort sizes are intentionally small to keep mentorship high-bandwidth.

Apply → Mentor inquiry →
02
Industry partnership

Take-Two DEI Partnership

Employment Internship pipeline Multi-year

A long-running partnership between GGP and Take-Two Interactive that creates direct internship and employment pathways for LGBTQ+ talent into Take-Two's family of studios — including Rockstar Games, 2K, and Zynga.

The partnership combines GGP's community network with Take-Two's hiring infrastructure: real roles, real pay, real resume. Not a recruiting day. A pipeline.

Student applications → Studios: replicate this →
03
Content library

Core Videos

Expertise Documentary On the record

A growing documentary library where queer industry leaders go on the record about craft, leadership, advancement, and survival in games. The conversations you don't get to hear on the main stage.

Core Videos is research-grade primary source material — for students, for HR teams, for journalists, for the historians who'll one day write the real history of this medium.

Watch the library → Suggest a guest →
04
Annual showcase

Collegiate Games Challenge

Education Showcase Annual

A national competition surfacing LGBTQ+ student game-makers. Teams submit playable demos; a jury of working industry pros reviews; finalists get visibility, mentor sessions, and a path into the GGP industry network.

CGC is a deliberate counter-program to the closed-door pipelines that have historically kept queer talent invisible during the formative student years.

Submit a project → Become a jury mentor →
05
Awards · annual

GGP Honors

Community Recognition Annual ceremony

The annual recognition of queer industry pros who moved the medium forward — on craft, on culture, on courage. Honors is a working ceremony. Honorees become permanent members of the GGP advisory network.

Sponsorship of GGP Honors directly funds scholarships and creator stipends across the rest of the program portfolio.

Sponsor Honors → Nominate a honoree →
06
Creator support

Twitch Affiliate Hub

Entrepreneurship Creator economy Ongoing

Onboarding, growth coaching, and platform support for LGBTQ+ streamers building paid careers on Twitch — and increasingly across YouTube and TikTok. We help creators clear the affiliate threshold, then push past it.

Topics covered include channel design, analytics, schedule discipline, brand deals, harassment defense, and the operational side of running yourself as a small business.
